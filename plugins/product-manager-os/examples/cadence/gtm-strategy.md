# GTM Strategy — engineering orgs, 20–200 people

**TL;DR**
- **Motion:** product-led with a light sales assist above 50 seats — because a $2,880 median ACV cannot carry a full sales cycle, and the buyer is an engineering manager with a corporate card, not procurement.
- **Beachhead:** distributed engineering teams of 20–200 at Series A–C companies. **Not yet:** enterprise (>500 seats) and non-engineering teams.
- **Channels:** SEO on async-standup intent, Slack App Directory, and champion-led internal expansion. **Loop:** every new team member invited is a new weekly active user who invites others.
- **Breaks first at 3x:** onboarding. Rollout already fails at 15+ seats and nothing in the current funnel notices.

## Economics (the constraint everything else obeys)
| | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ACV / price point | $2,880 median ($4/user/mo × 60 seats) | Stripe, trailing 90d |
| Gross margin | 84% | finance, Q2 |
| Max affordable CAC (12-mo payback) | **~$2,420** | derived: 2,880 × 0.84 |
| Current CAC | $1,910 blended | marketing spend / new logos |
| LTV:CAC | 3.1 | at 26-month median retention |
| Sales cycle | 19 days median, trial-led | CRM |

**What the economics rule out:** an outbound SDR motion. At a loaded cost of roughly $9k per closed deal at typical conversion, outbound loses money on every deal at this ACV and would need a 3× price increase to work. Field sales and channel partnerships are out for the same reason. What remains is self-serve plus an assist that costs a few hundred dollars per deal, not a few thousand.

## Motion
**Chosen:** product-led, sales-assisted above 50 seats. The product demos itself in a trial, the buyer can pay without approval up to about 80 seats, and the only expensive human moment is rollout — which is a customer-success job, not a sales job.

**Rejected:** enterprise sales-led — the ACV doesn't support it and there's no procurement gate to justify it. **Rejected:** pure self-serve with no humans — our own win/loss shows 11 of 17 losses died *after* a verbal yes, during rollout. Removing the human at exactly that moment would make the leak worse.

| Stage | Who does it | Human touch? | Target conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover → trial | Content + Slack directory | no | 8% |
| Trial → 5 posters in wk 1 | Product onboarding | no | 65% |
| 5 posters → paid | Self-serve checkout | no | 45% |
| 50+ seats | CS-led rollout call | yes, 1 call | 70% |

## Beachhead segment
- **Definition:** distributed engineering orgs of 20–200 people at Series A–C companies that already run async or hybrid, with Slack as the primary channel. Twenty real targets are nameable today.
- **Why them first:** they feel the standup cost acutely, they have a Slack habit to attach to, and the buyer signs without procurement.
- **Deliberately not yet:** enterprise above 500 seats — expand when we have SSO, an audit log, and two reference customers above 300 seats. Non-engineering teams (sales, support) — expand when engineering penetration in the beachhead exceeds 20%.

## Channels
| Channel | Why this buyer is there | Cost per qualified opp | Time to signal | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEO — "async standup", "standup alternative" | They search the moment standups get resented | $61 | 3–6 months | Marketing |

| *Rejected: paid search* | CPCs of $14+ on the head terms put CPA above the CAC ceiling | — | — | — |

## The loop
Every rollout adds team members; a share of those members become managers of their own teams or move companies and re-adopt. Currently worth about 0.4 new teams per rolled-out team per year — real, but under 1.0 it damps rather than compounds. Getting it above 1.0 is the single highest-value growth project we have, and it depends entirely on rollout succeeding.

## What breaks first
| At 3x volume | What fails | Early warning | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | Rollout already fails above 15 seats; nothing in the funnel detects it | Trials with <5 posters at day 3 | Automated rollout checklist, then CS trigger |
| CS capacity | One call per 50+ seat deal doesn't scale past ~40/month | Calls booked per CS head | Templatize the call, make it async-first |
| SEO | Head terms saturate; long tail is thin | Impressions flat while rankings hold | Expand into adjacent intent |

## Proof points and kill criteria
| Horizon | Works if | Stop if |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days | Slack-connected trials rise 34% → 55% | No movement — the placement isn't the problem |
| 60 days | Trials hitting 5 posters in wk 1 rise 41% → 60% | Below 45% — rollout is a product problem, not an onboarding one |
| 90 days | CAC holds under $2,000 with volume up 40% | CAC above $2,420 — the ceiling; pause spend and fix conversion |

## What we're explicitly not doing
- **Paid search** — CPCs put us above the CAC ceiling before a single trial converts.
- **Enterprise** — no SSO, no audit log, and a rollout motion that already fails at 15 seats. Fix the leak before widening the pipe.
- **Non-engineering teams** — the check-in format is built around eng workflow, and the demo doesn't land without it.
