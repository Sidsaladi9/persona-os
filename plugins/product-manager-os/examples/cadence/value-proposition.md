# Value Proposition — Cadence, engineering managers (20–200 person orgs)

**TL;DR**
- **Primary value:** managers stop spending their morning attention finding the one update that matters — Cadence surfaces it.
- **Strongest fit:** P1 (can't tell who's stuck until it's late) ← blocker detection. **Weakest link:** P2 (nobody reads what my team writes) has nothing pointed at it.
- **Fit verdict:** partial — we relieve the manager's severe pains and almost none of the writer's. That asymmetry is why teams churn in week 2.

**Segment:** engineering managers, 20–200 person orgs. Not the individual contributor — that's a separate canvas we have not run and should. **Evidence base:** 14 interviews (Apr–Jun 2026), 340 support tickets, 31 win/loss deals.

## The gaps that matter

**Severe pains with no reliever** *(roadmap input)*
- **P2 — the team writes and nobody visibly reads** (severity: high, week-2 survival 0.61). This is the churn mechanism and we have shipped nothing against it. A read receipt, a manager reaction, anything that closes the loop back to the writer would address it. **This is the highest-value unbuilt thing we have.**
- **P4 — rollout stalls above 15 people** (high, 11 of 17 losses). Not a product gap by intent, but the pain is real and severe and nothing addresses it.

**Relievers with no ranked pain** *(deprecation or research gap)*
- **Weekly summary email** — relieves nothing ranked, 4% open rate. Deprecate.
- **Searchable history** — serves only G4. Keep (cheap, occasionally decisive in enterprise deals) but stop featuring it in messaging.
