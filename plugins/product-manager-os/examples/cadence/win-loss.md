# Win/Loss — Q2 2026

**TL;DR**
- Deals die at the **admin-rollout stage**, not on price. 11 of 17 losses reached a verbal yes and then stalled during rollout to the wider team. 🔴 *(n=17 losses)*
- **"Too expensive" is not supported.** Losses requested a median 10% discount; wins requested 14%. The price objection appears where value wasn't established, not where the number was wrong. *(n=31)*
- **Slack-first teams win at 2.4×.** 13 of 14 wins had Slack connected before the trial ended; 5 of 17 losses did. 🟢
- The belief that we lose to Geekbot on features is not supported by the deal data.

**Sample:** 31 decided deals (14 won / 17 lost), 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30. **Confidence:** medium — n is adequate for the Slack finding, directional for the rest.

## Where deals die
| Stage | Wins reaching | Losses dying here | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| First demo | 14 | 2 | Positioning is working. Almost nobody bounces here. |
| Trial started | 14 | 4 | Trials start easily; the product demos well. |
| Verbal yes | 13 | **11** | 🔴 The failure is after the decision, during rollout. |
| Procurement | 8 | 0 | Not a bottleneck at our ACV. |

## Stated reasons vs. what the data shows
| Stated reason | Share of losses | Holds up? | What's actually going on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price / budget | 41% (7) | **no** | Wins asked for deeper discounts than losses did. |
| Went with competitor | 29% (5) | partly | 3 of 5 went to Geekbot; all 3 had failed rollout first. |
| No decision / timing | 24% (4) | yes | Champion never got the team writing check-ins. |
| Missing feature | 6% (1) | yes | One genuine Jira-integration loss. |

## Wins vs. losses on the same variables
| Variable | Wins | Losses | Difference real? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slack connected during trial | 93% (13/14) | 29% (5/17) | **yes** — largest gap in the data |
| ≥5 members posted in week 1 | 86% | 35% | yes |
| Economic buyer in first call | 57% | 47% | no — similar on both sides |
| Median discount requested | 14% | 10% | yes, and the opposite direction to the story |

## Findings

### 1. Rollout, not the sale, is where we lose
- **Evidence:** 11 of 17 losses reached a verbal yes. All 11 had fewer than 5 members posting by end of week 1.
- **Segment:** Concentrated in teams of 15+. Under 10 people, the champion just tells everyone in person.
- **Addressable?** Yes — onboarding, not product. One quarter.
- **Confidence:** high.

### 2. Slack connection is the single best leading indicator
- **Evidence:** 93% of wins vs 29% of losses. It's not that Slack is magic; it removes the "remember to go to a new tool" step that kills the habit in week one.
- **Addressable?** Yes, immediately — move Slack connection into the first-run flow rather than settings.
- **Confidence:** high.

### 3. Price objections are a symptom
- **Evidence:** discount depth runs the wrong way; every "too expensive" loss also had a failed rollout.
- **Addressable?** Indirectly — fix rollout and this bucket should shrink without touching the price.
- **Confidence:** medium — plausible mechanism, not proven causally.

## What we believed that isn't supported
- **"We're losing on price."** Wins negotiated harder than losses. The belief persisted because it's the dropdown reps pick when a deal goes quiet and there's nothing better to select.
- **"Geekbot is beating us on features."** All three Geekbot losses had already failed rollout. They didn't choose Geekbot over us; they chose anything over a tool their team wasn't using.

## Actions

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Move Slack connect into first-run, before first check-in | Priya | Share of trials with Slack connected in 48h: 34% → 70% | Aug 15 |
| 2 | Add a rollout checklist to the trial, triggered at day 3 if <5 posters | Priya | Trials reaching 5 posters in week 1: 41% → 65% | Aug 30 |
| 3 | Replace the CRM close-reason dropdown with "last stage reached" | Marcus (Sales) | 100% of Q3 losses have a stage recorded | Jul 15 |

## Limits of this analysis
- 31 deals in one quarter. The Slack finding is solid; the price finding is directional.
- Close reasons are rep-entered at close, when attention is lowest.
- Self-selection: we only see deals that reached our CRM. Teams that bounced off the website are invisible here.
- **Not asked:** we never spoke to a single lost buyer. Five 20-minute interviews with the stalled-rollout losses would settle finding 3 properly.
