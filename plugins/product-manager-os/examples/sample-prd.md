# Async Digest v2 — Product Brief

**Owner:** Priya Raman · **Status:** approved · **Updated:** 2026-07-28

## TL;DR
- Cadence replaces synchronous standups with async written check-ins plus an AI digest for managers.
- ~4,000 paying teams, ~$3M ARR, $4/user/mo on Team plus custom enterprise.
- Our #1 problem is week-2 retention: 39% of teams that check in during week 1 never return a qualifying week 2.
- North star: weekly active teams posting check-ins on ≥3 days that week. Currently 1,420 of ~4,000.

## Who it's for
Engineering managers running distributed teams of 20–200 at Series A–C companies, already on Slack. Not teams under 10 — they're in one room and don't need us.

## The problem
Async check-ins solved the standup meeting and created a reading problem. A manager with eleven reports gets eleven paragraphs every morning and the one that matters is buried in the seventh. Median digest dwell time is 40 seconds against a 71% open rate.

## This quarter
Ship the AI Blocker Detector: read the check-ins teams already write and surface the ones describing a blocker, at the top of the digest, in the person's own words. Target: 60% of teams see at least one real blocker in week 1, dismissal rate under 25%.

## How we work
Two-week sprints, quarterly OKRs. Sole PM, 6 engineers, 1 designer. Metrics review Fridays; leadership update goes out the same afternoon.

## House style
Every doc opens with a TL;DR in bullets. Sentence-case headings. We say "teams", never "customers". Status is 🟢🟡🔴, never RAG or percentages.
